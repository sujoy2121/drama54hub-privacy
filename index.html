Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Drama54Hub ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our social media publishing and content management application.

Our application is designed to help manage and publish content to connected social media accounts and pages, including Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.

Important: We do not sell personal information to third parties.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the services and accounts you connect, our application may process the following types of information:

  • Social media account identifiers and page/channel identifiers.
  • Account, Page, or Channel names and publicly available account information.
  • Authentication information and access credentials required to connect authorized social media accounts.
  • Videos, images, thumbnails, captions, titles, descriptions, hashtags, and other content that you choose to upload or publish.
  • Publishing schedules and publication status.
  • Information required to retrieve publishing status, analytics, comments, or other supported social media functionality when such features are enabled.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application may be used for the following purposes:

  • Connecting authorized social media accounts.
  • Uploading and publishing content.
  • Scheduling posts and other supported content.
  • Checking publishing and processing status.
  • Managing media and content metadata.
  • Providing supported analytics, engagement, or management functionality.
  • Maintaining the security, reliability, and performance of the application.
  • Troubleshooting errors and improving application functionality.

3. Facebook and Instagram

If you connect Facebook or Instagram accounts, the application accesses information and permissions only as necessary for the functionality you authorize.

This may include the ability to manage content publishing, retrieve publishing status, and use other Facebook or Instagram features that you explicitly authorize.

Access to Facebook and Instagram information is subject to the permissions granted through Meta's authorization process.

We do not use Facebook or Instagram information for purposes unrelated to the functionality provided by the application.

4. YouTube

If you connect a YouTube account, the application may use the permissions you authorize to upload, schedule, manage, or retrieve information about content through the YouTube API.

YouTube account information is handled according to the permissions granted during Google's OAuth authorization process.

5. Authentication and Access Tokens

When you authorize a social media platform, the platform may provide authentication credentials or access tokens that allow the application to perform the actions you have authorized.

These credentials are used only for authorized application functionality and should not be shared publicly.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect authentication information from unauthorized access.

Security notice: Never publish access tokens, client secrets, API keys, or other authentication credentials in public repositories, screenshots, or public messages.

6. Media and Content

Content that you upload to the application may be temporarily processed or stored to perform requested publishing operations.

This may include videos, images, thumbnails, captions, titles, descriptions, hashtags, and scheduling information.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights and permissions to upload and publish the content you provide.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when this is necessary to provide functionality that you have requested. These services may include social media platforms and infrastructure or storage providers used by the application.

For example, when you choose to publish a video to Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube, the relevant content and information must be transmitted to that platform to complete the requested operation.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain publishing records, troubleshoot issues, maintain security, or comply with applicable obligations.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the functionality being used.

9. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of the application.

You may also disconnect an authorized social media account from the application. Depending on the platform, you may revoke the application's permissions directly through the relevant platform's account settings.

To request deletion of information associated with Drama54Hub, contact us using the contact information provided below.

Deletion request: Please include the account or Page name associated with your request so that we can identify the relevant information.

10. Third-Party Services

Our application may interact with third-party services such as:

  • Meta / Facebook
  • Instagram
  • Google / YouTube
  • Cloud storage or media processing services
  • Other services required to provide requested functionality

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the services you connect to the application.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The application may use cookies or similar technologies required for authentication, security, session management, or basic website functionality.

We do not use cookies for the purpose of selling personal information.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's Privacy

The application is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, services, legal requirements, or security practices.

When changes are made, the updated date at the top of this page will be changed accordingly.
